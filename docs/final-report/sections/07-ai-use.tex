\section{AI Tool Use}
We used ChatGPT/Codex for implementation suggestions, debugging, tests,
experiment scripts, analysis, literature leads, and English/LaTeX editing,
including drafts of the abstract and Sections~4--6. The authors manually
revised and checked Section~4 and the main-study E1--E3 results in Section~5.

\paragraph{Implementation and testing.}
AI assisted storage, repair, Controller/playback integration, and console
development. Changes were checked through builds, static checks, race tests,
and system tests covering publication, integrity, repair, cleanup, deletion,
and media delivery; archived executions provide the implementation evidence.

\paragraph{Experiments and analysis.}
AI helped with fault schedules, instrumentation, aggregation, and tables.
Measurements came from real processes and HTTP transfers, checked against
injection journals, session events, traffic counters, task histories, and
verified committed replicas. Exploratory runs and validation remain
separate; anomalies and replacements are documented, and application
failures remain in the denominators.

\paragraph{Writing and responsibility.}
References and paper-specific claims were checked against source documents,
and numerical claims against experimental records. The authors retain
responsibility for the code, references, analysis, and interpretation.
