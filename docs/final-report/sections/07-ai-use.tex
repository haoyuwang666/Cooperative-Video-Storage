\section{AI Tool Use}
We used ChatGPT/Codex throughout development and report preparation.
Its roles included proposing implementation changes, explaining failures,
drafting tests and experiment scripts, suggesting analysis and literature
search directions, and editing English and LaTeX. AI also assisted in
drafting the abstract and Sections~4--6. The authors manually revised and
checked Section~4 and the main-study E1--E3 results in Section~5.

\paragraph{Implementation and testing.}
AI assistance covered storage and repair logic, Controller and playback
integration, and the management console. Proposed changes were checked
against the implementation and exercised through builds, static checks,
race tests, and targeted system tests. The archived checks include
publication, integrity, repair, cleanup, deletion, and media validation.
These executable checks, rather than a model's description of intended
behavior, provided the implementation evidence used in the report.

\paragraph{Experiments and analysis.}
AI helped construct fault schedules, instrumentation, aggregation scripts,
and result tables. Numerical results came from actual processes and HTTP
transfers, not language-model-generated measurements. Verification used
recorded injections, session events, traffic counters, task histories,
and hash-valid committed replica checks. Exploratory runs, independent
validation, and completion-gated controls remain distinct. Measurement
anomalies and replacement runs are documented; application failures remain
in the reported denominators. This distinction is particularly important
when a plausible explanation differs from what a trace actually records.

\paragraph{Writing and responsibility.}
AI supplied literature leads, wording, figure and table formatting, and
LaTeX debugging assistance. Bibliographic details and paper-specific claims
were checked against source documents, while quantitative statements were
traced to experimental records. Repository history and retained artifacts
support review of these changes. The authors remain responsible for the
submitted code, references, analysis, and final interpretation.
